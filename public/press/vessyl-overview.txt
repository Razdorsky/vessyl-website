The Vessyl

The Vessyl is an immersive wellness destination located on 118 acres of tropical rainforest near the Arenal Volcano in La Fortuna, Costa Rica. Founded by Charlotte's Web co-founder Josh Stanley and developed in partnership with AKEN Hotels & Resorts, The Vessyl combines sound, nature, restorative experiences, and intentional community to support presence, recovery, and meaningful human connection.

Vessyl was founded by entrepreneur Josh Stanley, co-founder of Charlotte's Web.

At the heart of The Vessyl is the Frequency Dome, an immersive sound sanctuary designed to support relaxation and restoration.

Music is central to the Vessyl philosophy. Programming may include intimate performances and immersive experiences designed to foster creativity, presence, and connection.

At Rancho, The Vessyl's signature poolside restaurant, guests gather beneath the shadow of Volcano Arenal to experience the flavors, ingredients, and traditions that define Costa Rica.

The Vessyl is located near La Fortuna, Costa Rica, in the shadow of the Arenal Volcano.
